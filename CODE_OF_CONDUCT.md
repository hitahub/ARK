# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the ARC CLI project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and feedback without attacking people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work toward resolving disagreements respectfully.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project communication.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be sent privately to a project maintainer. If no private reporting channel is published, contact a maintainer through an available project channel and request a private conversation. Maintainers will review reports and take action they consider appropriate to the circumstances.
